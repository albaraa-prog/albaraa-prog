### About me

I've been taking things apart in my head since I was a kid. Put something in, and what comes out, and why? By grade 12 I knew software was where I could answer that question every day.

I still can't stay in one lane. I jump between **AI, mobile apps, games, 3D and video editing**, and each one makes me better at the others. Today I lead a dev team at **Digital Dimension** in Muscat, and I build AI, apps and games with Oman in them, in Arabic and English.

- 💼 **Software Development Manager** at Digital Dimension
- 🎓 **B.Sc. (Hons) Computer Science**, Middle East College · Coventry University (GPA 3.5), Technical Lead at Google Developer Group
- 📄 Published at **ICADAAI 2026** · ★ **Best Project Poster Award**, Middle East College, Fall 2025
- 🎮 After hours: Rust, CS2 and building worlds in Minecraft, all feeding one goal, **a video game set in Oman**
- 🗣️ Arabic (native) and English (fluent)
